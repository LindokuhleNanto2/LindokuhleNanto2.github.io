Designing a brighter start for young English learners.

This portfolio documents the planning, design, development support and reflection behind BrightStart English Tutoring, a web-based learning service designed to support South African learners in Grades 1–3 with English literacy.

Prepared by Lindokuhle Nanto · UI/UX Designer · CPUT

Project at a glance

The purpose, users and learning goals behind the work.

01

Who it supports

Young learners in Grades 1–3, their parents or guardians, and tutors who support literacy development.

02

What it offers

English learning support through reading, writing, comprehension, simple quizzes, session bookings and learner-progress tracking.

03

How we organised work

Our team used Trello as the project taskboard, with a Kanban-style workflow to organise tasks, ownership and progress.

My role in the project

My role in the team charter was UI/UX Designer. I worked on the look, structure and usability of the website by creating Figma screen designs. I designed the login and account-creation screens and also contributed to development troubleshooting when we encountered a payment error. In addition, I helped check whether the frontend and backend integrated and worked together as expected.
